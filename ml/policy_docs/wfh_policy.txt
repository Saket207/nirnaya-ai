================================================================================
               NIRNAYA ENTERPRISE - WORK FROM HOME & HYBRID POLICY
================================================================================
Policy Code: POL-OPS-002
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applies To: All Engineering, Product, Business, and Corporate Staff

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: HYBRID WORK MODEL & OFFICE PRESENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Hybrid Baseline Schedule
- Employees are required to work 3 days per week from their designated office and 2 days remotely.
- Tuesday and Thursday are company-wide mandatory in-office anchor days.

1.2 Core Availability Hours
- All employees must maintain availability during core hours: 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time.

1.3 Fully Remote Work Eligibility
- Full-time remote work requires at least 18 months of tenure with consistently strong performance evaluations, or a verified medical/relocation exception approved by People Operations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: HOME OFFICE SETUP & INFRASTRUCTURE STIPEND
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Equipment Allowance
- Employees past their 90-day probation are eligible for a one-time home office reimbursement of up to $600.

2.2 Monthly Connectivity Allowance
- Active remote and hybrid employees receive a monthly stipend of $50 for broadband and connectivity costs.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: DATA PRIVACY AND SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Approved Hardware
- All work must be done on company-provisioned, MDM-managed laptops.

3.2 Secure Network
- Connecting to unsecured public Wi-Fi without the company VPN is a compliance violation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: WORKLOAD BALANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Right to Disconnect
- Employees are encouraged to disconnect from work communications outside their 8-hour workday.
