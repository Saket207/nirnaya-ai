SECTION 1: WORKPLACE CULTURE & ETHICAL CONDUCT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Zero-Tolerance Anti-Harassment & Non-Discrimination
- Acme Corp maintains zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or bullying based on race, gender, religion, caste, national origin, sexual orientation, disability, or age.
- Any retaliatory action taken against an employee who reports a code violation or participates in an investigation will result in immediate termination for cause.

1.2 Conflict of Interest & Dual Employment (Moonlighting)
- Full-time employees may not hold outside commercial employment, operate competitive business entities, or render consulting services for external vendors without explicit written clearance from the Legal and People Operations teams.
- Open-source contributions, non-profit volunteer work, and personal creative writing are permitted provided they occur outside core working hours and use personal hardware.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: CAREER PROGRESSION, STAGNATION & RETENTION INTERVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Promotion Criteria and Stagnation Guardrails
- Formal promotion cycles occur twice annually (June Mid-Year and December Year-End).
- Stagnation Warning Trigger: Any high-performing individual contributor who has completed 24 consecutive months in the same role grade without promotion or title progression must be reviewed for leveling calibration by the Department Head.

2.2 Retention Bonus Protocols (Flight Risk Mitigation)
- Target Audience: High-performing employees identified as High Flight Risk whose attrition would materially impact business continuity.
- Monetary Ceilings & Rules:
  * Maximum Retention Bonus: Capped at 15% of the employee's current annual base salary.
  * Standard Offer (5% to 10%): Requires approval from the direct Manager and Department Head.
  * Exceptional Offer (10.1% to 15%): Requires dual executive approval from the Chief Human Resources Officer (CHRO) and Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
- Vesting & Repayment Obligations:
  * The retention bonus is disbursed as a lump sum paired with a binding 12-month service commitment agreement.
  * If the employee resigns voluntarily or is terminated for misconduct within 12 months of payout, 100% of the retention bonus must be repaid to the company within 30 days of the last working day.

2.3 Role Transitions and Internal Mobility
- Eligibility: Employees who have served at least 9 months in their current role with a performance rating of 3.0/5.0 or higher are eligible to apply for internal job transfers across different teams or technical tracks.
- Lateral Role Resets: Managers cannot block an internal transfer once an offer is extended by the receiving team, though a transition buffer of up to 30 calendar days may be negotiated.
- Exploratory 15% "Project-Swap": To alleviate role fatigue and turnover risk, a manager may authorize an employee to commit up to 15% of their working capacity (6 hours/week) to a secondary cross-functional project for up to 90 days.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT PLANS (PIP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 PIP Triggers and Timelines
- When an employee exhibits persistent performance deficits or scores below 2.5/5.0 in review cycles, a structured Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) lasting 30 or 60 days may be instituted.
- The PIP document must outline:
  * Specific, quantifiable weekly milestones.
  * Dedicated mentorship (minimum of one 45-minute structured 1:1 check-in per week).
  * Objective pass/fail criteria agreed upon by the Manager and HRBP.

3.2 Consequences & Protections During PIP
- While enrolled in an active PIP, an employee is ineligible for merit salary raises, internal transfers, retention bonuses, or company-sponsored travel.
- Successful completion closes the inquiry; failure to meet measurable targets results in structured mutual separation with standard severance.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: GRIEVANCE REDRESSAL & WHISTLEBLOWER ESCALATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Escalation Matrix
- Level 1: Informal resolution with Direct Manager within 5 business days.
- Level 2: Formal escalation to the assigned HRBP with mandatory written response within 7 business days.
- Level 3: Anonymous Whistleblower Hotline for severe ethical breaches, accounting fraud, or executive misconduct, investigated directly by the Board Audit Committee.
