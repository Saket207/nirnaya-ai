              ACME CORP BUSINESS EXPENSES & LEARNING STIPENDS
================================================================================
Document ID: POL-FIN-EXP-2026-V1
Classification: Financial & Operating Governance
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: All Full-Time Corporate Staff

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: TRAVEL, LODGING, AND DAILY PER DIEM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Domestic Flight & Rail Travel
- Air travel is permitted for verified business trips exceeding 300 kilometers.
- Flight Class: Economy / Coach class is mandatory for all domestic flights and short-haul international flights under 6 hours.
- Bookings must be initiated through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days prior to departure to secure optimal corporate rates.

1.2 Hotel Accommodations & Lodging Caps
- Tier 1 Cities (Metro hubs: New York, London, Tokyo, Bengaluru, Singapore): Up to $180 per night, taxes included.
- Tier 2/3 Cities (Regional centers): Up to $110 per night, taxes included.
- Mini-bar, pay-per-view movies, and personal laundry services are strictly non-reimbursable.

1.3 Meals and Incidentals (Per Diem)
- When traveling on approved business, employees may claim meals up to $60 per day without prior authorization:
  * Breakfast: Up to $15
  * Lunch: Up to $20
  * Dinner: Up to $25
- Alcohol consumption cannot be reimbursed under individual per diem allocations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: PROFESSIONAL LEARNING & CERTIFICATION BUDGET
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Annual Professional Development Allowance
- Every full-time employee with more than 3 months of service is entitled to an annual Learning & Development (L&D) budget of up to $1,200 per calendar year.
- Approved Expenditures:
  * Technical cloud/security certifications (AWS, GCP, CISA, CKAD)
  * Industry conference passes and workshops
  * Technical publications, books, and paid online course platforms (Coursera, Udemy, O'Reilly)

2.2 Approval & Clawback Provision
- Pre-approval from the direct engineering or department manager is mandatory prior to purchase.
- Retention Commitment: If an employee leaves the organization voluntarily within 6 months of receiving L&D funding exceeding $800, 50% of the reimbursed sum is subject to clawback from the final settlement.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: TEAM BONDING, RETENTION, AND MORALE EVENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Team Collaboration Budget
- Engineering and product managers are allocated a quarterly morale budget of $75 per direct report to host team-building lunches, hackathon celebratory dinners, or off-site collaborative outings.
- Morale budgets cannot be converted into individual gift cards or rolled over across fiscal years.

3.2 Milestone Recognition Awards
- Spot awards up to $250 can be granted by Department Directors for exceptional project contributions, critical bug patches, or zero-downtime migrations.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: SUBMISSION, APPROVALS, AND AUDIT CYCLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Expense Submission Deadlines
- Expense claims must be submitted via the finance portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction date. Claims submitted after 45 days will be permanently denied.
- Original itemized tax invoices/receipts are mandatory for all transactions exceeding $15.

4.2 Approval Thresholds
- Claims up to $500: Direct Manager approval.
- Claims between $501 and $2,500: Department Head approval.
- Claims exceeding $2,500: Department Head + Finance Controller dual sign-off.
