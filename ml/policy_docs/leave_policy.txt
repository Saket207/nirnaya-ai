================================================================================
                    ACME CORP LEAVE POLICY
================================================================================
Document ID: POL-HR-LEAVE-2026-V1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Applicability: All Full-Time Employees

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: ANNUAL LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1 Entitlement
- Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
- Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 1.5 days per month.

1.2 Carry Forward
- Unused annual leave can be carried forward into the next calendar year, up to a maximum of 10 days.
- Any unused leave beyond this cap is forfeited at year-end.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: SICK LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 Entitlement
- Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per calendar year.
- Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year.

2.2 Medical Certificate Requirement
- A medical certificate is required for any sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days.
- Sick leave for 1-3 days can be self-certified without documentation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: MATERNITY AND PATERNITY LEAVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 Maternity Leave
- Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave.

3.2 Paternity Leave
- Eligible employees are entitled to 2 weeks of paid paternity leave, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 4: LEAVE APPLICATION PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1 Advance Notice
- All planned leave requests (annual leave) must be submitted at least 3 working days in advance through the HR portal.
- Sick leave is exempt from advance notice and should be reported to the manager as soon as possible.

4.2 Approval
- Leave requests are approved by the employee's direct manager within 2 working days of submission.
